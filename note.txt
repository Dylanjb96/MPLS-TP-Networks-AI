Phase 2:
The two 12-node configurations illustrate why gateway architecture matters.
The single-gateway network remains connected after individual physical-link removals but fails the node-loss connectivity diagnostic. The dual-gateway network passes both.
This suggests that the single-gateway architecture introduces a potential interconnection single point of failure.
However, these are graph-connectivity results, not demonstrations of actual MPLS-TP service recovery or protection switching.
Likewise, generating networks with 60, 100 and 150 nodes demonstrates that the topology generator supports those sizes. It does not yet establish the computational scalability of the actual existing system.
